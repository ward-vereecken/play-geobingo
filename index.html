Short lessons, real progression

The lessons are quick, but around them sits a full loop of daily rewards, journey progression, retries, revision, redemption, streaks, collections, and rankings.

Visual learning

GeoBingo is built around maps, regions, borders, and placement. It feels closer to building a mental atlas than memorizing flashcards.

Progress you can see

As your GeoDex fills up with country knowledge and fun facts, the app keeps revisiting mistakes and old material so learning compounds over time.

What you can learn

A lot more than flags and capitals.

The goal of GeoBingo is to teach you geography of the entire world, from all sides.
